Upoznaj me

Osoba iza objektiva

Ja sam Ivona Šarić - supruga svog najboljeg prijatelja iz osnovne škole, mama djevojčice krupnih smeđih očiju i geolog s fotoaparatom u ruci.

Volim ljude, njihove priče i hvatanje iskrenih osmijaha, poljubaca i zagrljaja. Kroz fotografiju ulazim u svjetove osoba koje fotografiram i na trenutak postajem svjedok njihove ljubavi. Moj cilj je zabilježiti te emocije i sačuvati ih kao trajnu uspomenu.

Zbog toga osjećam ogromnu odgovornost, zahvalnost i privilegiju - jer svatko od vas je poseban i ima svoje mjesto u mom srcu.

I'm Ivona Šarić - the wife of my best friend from elementary school, the mother of a girl with big brown eyes, and a geologist with a camera in her hand.

I love people, their stories, and capturing genuine smiles, kisses, and hugs. Through photography, I step into the worlds of the people I photograph and, for a moment, become a witness to their love. My goal is to capture those emotions and preserve them as a lasting memory.

Because of this, I feel immense responsibility, gratitude, and privilege - because every one of you is special and has a place in my heart.
